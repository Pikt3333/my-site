Residential Locksmith Service

Dr. Keys is a residential locksmith for homes, condos and apartments in Aventura, Sunny Isles Beach, North Miami, North Miami Beach, Hallandale Beach, Hollywood and Fort Lauderdale. We help when you're locked out of your house, and when your locks need to be changed, repaired or rekeyed.

Locked out of your house? We open the door with professional tools, without damaging the door or frame whenever possible. We'll ask for ID showing you live at the address.

Change locks or rekey? Just moved in, lost a key, or a roommate moved out? Rekeying changes the inside of your existing lock so old keys stop working — usually cheaper than buying new locks. Lock change replaces the lock completely — the right choice when the lock is worn out, damaged, or you want a stronger deadbolt.

We also repair locks that stick or won't turn, and install new door locks and deadbolts. Price on the phone before we come.
