Car Key Replacement at Your Location

Lost your only car key, or it broke in the lock? You don't need to tow the car to a dealer and wait days. Dr. Keys is a mobile car locksmith: we come to your home, office or parking lot in Aventura, Hollywood, North Miami, Sunny Isles Beach and nearby, cut a new key from the car itself, and program it to your vehicle on the spot.

No original key? Not a problem. Losing all your keys ("all keys lost") is the most common call we get. We decode the lock, cut a new key with a precision key-cutting machine, and program the chip with professional programming tools. When we leave, the car starts and the key is yours.

We also make spare keys while you still have one that works — the cheapest and fastest moment to do it. Having just one key is how most "all keys lost" situations start.
