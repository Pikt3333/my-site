Key Won't Turn in the Ignition?

When the key won't turn, gets stuck, or the car cranks but won't start, the problem is usually either the key (worn, bent, or its chip lost programming) or the ignition cylinder (worn inside). Dr. Keys diagnoses it at your car in Aventura, Hollywood, North Miami, Sunny Isles Beach and nearby, and fixes it on site when possible.

Before you call, try this: (1) Gently turn the steering wheel left and right while turning the key — the steering lock may be holding the ignition. (2) Make sure the car is fully in Park (or Neutral). (3) Try your spare key. If the spare works, the problem is the key, not the ignition — and a new key is cheaper than an ignition repair.

Car in anti-theft mode? If the security light blinks and the engine won't start, the car isn't recognizing the chip in your key. Often the key needs to be reprogrammed or replaced — something we do on site.
